We’re continuing our series on collaboration, from start to finish—in part three, we’re shining the spotlight to the dynamic duo who (somehow) make sense of our messy drafts: beta readers and editors! We’ll dig into the most common challenges writers face during early and late-stage revisions, share insights from writers, betas, and editors in the community, and consider ways to navigate the process with confidence and clarity.
