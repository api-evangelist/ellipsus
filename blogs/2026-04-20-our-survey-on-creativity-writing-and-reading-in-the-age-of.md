What does it actually feel like to be a writer in the age of AI? For nearly three years, we’ve been building Ellipsus alongside a growing community of writers. And in that time, AI has shifted from background noise to an unavoidable force shaping the tools, platforms, and economies of writing—affecting almost every aspect of the creative process.
